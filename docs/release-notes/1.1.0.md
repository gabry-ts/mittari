Mittari now shares one design with Tuuli, Kaiku, Kiito and Kello. The popover, the statistics window, Settings and About are built from the same components as the other apps, so buttons, switches, cards and the settings window look and behave the same everywhere.

The popover keeps the ring gauge, the 5-hour window, the week, today and this month, models, projects and Codex, on a lighter glass background. A new footer has Statistics, Settings, a ⋯ menu for updates and Buy Me a Coffee, and Quit. The menu bar item uses the same type as the other apps.

The statistics window and Settings now live in one window with a sidebar. The updates switch and the version moved to About.

Nothing changes in what Mittari reads from your Mac, and no new permissions are needed.
